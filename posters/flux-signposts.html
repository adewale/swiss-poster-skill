Four lenses for a curve

FLUX never made solar a lens, but four of its lenses fit the solar thread: growth that meets a ceiling, a loop that compounds, efficiency that creates demand, and layers that move at different speeds.

Ep. 02 · May 2021

the S-curve

“Any process with positive feedback loops and growth ceilings will end up exhibiting sigmoid, or ‘S,’ curves… The sooner we accept that the shape of this curve is set, the more nimble we can be about finding the next one.”

Ep. 34 · Jan 2022

compounding loops

“Every time we see a metric line starting to curve, chances are we’re seeing a compounding loop at work… After all, it’s better to ride the compounding loop than be ridden by it.”

Ep. 184 · Apr 2025

Jevons paradox

“Efficiency reduces the cost of action, which can expand the domain of action itself… When evaluating improvements, ask not just what they save, but what they enable.”

Ep. 74 · Nov 2022

Pace Layers

“Fast-adapting layers act as buffers to help absorb change, only letting changes filter down to lower layers if they stand the test of time and scale.”
