Signposts

Every issue opens its Signposts with “clues that point to where our changing world might lead us.” Sorted into eight topics, the 1,205 clues show what the weeks added up to. In 2021 the biggest topic was Money: lumber, house prices, Dogecoin. From 2022 AI was first and stayed first. Energy climbed from sixth to second, 7% of the clues to 23%, and solar power came with it.

Each Signpost gets the same angle, so a wide wedge is a busy quarter; bands are the quarter’s topic mix, Government at the hub, Energy at the rim.
